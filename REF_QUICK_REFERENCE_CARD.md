# REF Impact Case Study - Quick Reference Card

## 🎯 The Three Pillars of REF Impact

| Pillar | Questions to Answer | Red Flags | Green Flags |
|--------|-------------------|-----------|------------|
| **REACH** | Who benefited? How many? Where? | ❌ "Several org" ❌ Unknown count ❌ Vague location | ✅ "500+ patients" ✅ "12 hospitals" ✅ "UK-wide" |
| **SIGNIFICANCE** | How much changed? By how much? For how long? | ❌ No numbers ❌ "Improved" ❌ One-time event | ✅ "40% faster" ✅ "£2M savings" ✅ "12+ months" |
| **EVIDENCE** | How do we know? Is it verified? | ❌ Vendor claims ❌ Unverifiable ❌ Internal only | ✅ Published paper ✅ Policy doc ✅ 3rd party verify |

---

## 📝 Write Better REF Prompts

### Formula
```
[Topic] + [Real-World Use] + [Quantified Outcome] + [Beneficiary Type] + [Timeframe]
```

### Bad Example ❌
"AI in healthcare"

### Good Example ✅
"AI diagnostic tools that improved patient outcomes (>30% accuracy gain) adopted 
by healthcare providers (>5 hospitals), reaching 100+ patients, with published case 
studies or peer-reviewed evidence, implemented 2023-2024"

## 💾 REF Metric Examples

### Economic Impact
- Cost savings: "£500,000 in first year"
- Revenue growth: "15% increase in market share"
- Jobs: "50 new positions created"
- Adoption: "Adopted by 20+ companies"

### Health Impact
- Patient outcomes: "40% improvement in survival rate"
- Access: "Reached 5,000+ underserved patients"
- Safety: "30% reduction in medical errors"
- Efficiency: "2-hour to 30-minute diagnosis time"

### Social Impact
- Reach: "1 million citizens affected"
- Accessibility: "75% increase in user access"
- Inclusion: "Benefited 10,000+ underrepresented groups"
- Behavior change: "65% of participants adopted new practice"

### Policy Impact
- Adoption: "Policy adopted by 27 EU member states"
- Regulation: "Influenced new government regulations"
- Funding: "Triggered £50M in government investment"
- Scale: "Affects 100+ million people"

### Environmental Impact
- Reduction: "500,000 tonnes CO2 saved annually"
- Cost: "20% resource cost reduction"
- Adoption: "Implemented by 50+ organizations"
- Duration: "Sustained for 3+ years"

## ✅ REF Compliance Checklist

Before submitting a case study, verify:

**REACH**
- [ ] Named external organization (not just internal)
- [ ] Specific number of beneficiaries (not "several")
- [ ] Geographic location stated
- [ ] Beneficiary type identified

**SIGNIFICANCE**
- [ ] At least one quantified metric (%, £, #, time)
- [ ] Metric is meaningful (>5% or equivalent)
- [ ] Sustained impact (>6-12 months evidence)
- [ ] Real-world change described

**EVIDENCE**
- [ ] Published or documented (link or citation)
- [ ] Independent verification (3rd party)
- [ ] Source is credible (academic, policy, media, verified)
- [ ] Recent (within 2-3 years)

**BEYOND-ACADEMIA**
- [ ] External beneficiary (not just your team)
- [ ] Real-world application (not just theory)
- [ ] Verifiable impact (not claimed/projected)

**All YES?** ✅ Ready for REF!

## 🚫 Common REF Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Example | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Too vague | "Used by an organization" | "Used by 50 NHS hospitals" |
| No numbers | "Improved efficiency" | "Reduced processing time 75% (2hr→30min)" |
| Only internal | "Our team adopted it" | "10,000 external users benefited" |
| Only academic | "Published a paper" | "Policy adopted affecting 1M people" |
| Unverifiable | "Reportedly helped" | "Documented in case study (link)" |

## 📊 Scoring Framework (REF Alignment)

```
Score 9-10: Excellent
- Clear beneficiary identification
- Significant quantified outcome (>20% improvement)
- Multi-site/international reach
- Peer-reviewed or policy-documented
- 2+ years sustained impact

Score 7-8: Good
- Named beneficiary with count
- Meaningful outcome metric (5-20% improvement)
- Regional/national reach
- Published or official documentation
- 12+ months impact

Score 5-6: Moderate
- Beneficiary type identified
- Some quantified outcome
- Limited geographic reach
- Documented but not peer-reviewed
- 6-12 months impact

Score 3-4: Weak
- Vague beneficiary
- Limited/no metrics
- Internal or unclear scope
- Unverified sources
- <6 months impact

Score 1-2: Reject
- No clear beneficiary
- No quantified outcome
- Internal-only impact
- No verifiable evidence
- Purely academic
```

## 🎓 Impact Type Quick Reference

Choose the primary impact type for your case study:

| Type | Examples | Key Metrics |
|------|----------|------------|
| **Economic** | Cost savings, revenue growth, jobs, market adoption | % savings, £ value, # jobs, adoption rate |
| **Health** | Clinical outcomes, patient access, preventive care | % improvement, # patients, outcomes, safety |
| **Social** | Inclusion, accessibility, behavior change, quality of life | # beneficiaries, % adoption, inclusion metrics |
| **Environmental** | Emissions, conservation, resource reduction | Tonnes CO2, % reduction, # organizations |
| **Policy** | Government adoption, regulation, funding triggered | # policies, jurisdiction size, implementation |
| **Cultural** | Public engagement, heritage, awareness | Reach, participation, media coverage |
| **Education** | Learning outcomes, progression, accessibility | # students, % improvement, accessibility % |

## 💡 Pro Tips

✅ Always include 3+ quantified metrics  
✅ Name specific organizations and locations  
✅ Include 2+ independent sources  
✅ Show how impact was sustained (>12 months)  
✅ Clearly separate who benefited from how much  
✅ Use recent examples (2-3 years)  
✅ Focus on real-world change, not just activity  

❌ Avoid vague language ("improved", "helped", "benefited")  
❌ Don't claim impact without evidence  
❌ Don't count internal-only benefits  
❌ Don't rely on marketing materials  
❌ Don't ignore geographic scope  
❌ Don't forget to include beneficiary count
